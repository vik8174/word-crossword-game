## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Development process setup: ADRs, CONTRIBUTING, CI plan
- Repository scaffolding: pnpm workspace (`apps/web`, `packages/shared`), Vite + React + React Router + MUI, Firebase SDK (env config), ESLint + Prettier, Vitest with an 80% coverage threshold, GitHub Actions CI (lint/test/coverage) ([issue #2](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/2))
- CI `Build` job (`pnpm build` for both packages), and the workflow now also runs on `push` to `main`, not only on pull requests ([issue #17](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/17))
- `apps/web/src/firebase/config.ts` fails immediately, listing every missing required `VITE_FIREBASE_*` variable, instead of passing `undefined` into `initializeApp` ([issue #17](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/17))
- `crossword-generator` module in `packages/shared`: `generateCrossword(words)` builds an intersecting crossword grid and returns the words that did not fit as `unplacedWords` instead of losing them ([issue #3](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/3), [ADR 0008](docs/decisions/0008-crossword-layout-library-and-contract.md))
- `word-list-validator` module in `packages/shared`: turns the raw text of the word-list field into the words a game is built from, plus every reason the list cannot start one — count, length, alphabet and duplicates ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4))
- Room creation, end to end: the owner enters a nickname and a word list, the crossword is generated in the browser, the room is written to Firestore with the owner already in it as its first player, and the owner gets a link to share ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4), [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-room-document-schema.md))
- Words that did not fit into the grid are shown to the owner for approval before the room is created; a word list that yields no grid at all is refused with an explanation instead of becoming an unplayable room ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4))
- Joining a room by its link, end to end: the visitor is signed in anonymously the moment the page opens — before the room is read, as the security rules require — then picks a nickname and is written into the room's `players` map under their own auth id. The screen follows the room live from then on, so the player list fills in as the others arrive ([issue #5](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/5), [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-room-document-schema.md))
- The room screen draws the crossword as bare squares, with no letters: the room document holds every word in plain text, and nothing may be shown until words are assigned to players ([issue #5](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/5), [ADR 0004](docs/decisions/0004-ui-only-word-visibility.md))
- A link that leads nowhere is explained rather than left blank: an unknown address, a room that was never there or has already been collected, a room past its 24 hours, and a room that already holds four players each say what happened and offer a way on ([issue #5](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/5))
- `word-assignment` module in `packages/shared`: `assignWords({ wordCount, playerIds })` deals a crossword's words out among 2-4 players, hiding each word from exactly one of them and spreading them evenly — including who carries the spare when the words do not divide evenly. Everybody is left at least one word to guess: a crossword with fewer words than players is refused rather than dealt, and `wordAssignmentRefusal` states that condition once, so the start button explains what is missing instead of offering a deal that would throw ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- Starting a game, end to end: the owner deals the words out from two players up, the assignment is written into `words.<id>.hiddenFromPlayerId` in one update, and every screen picks the same deal up from its live subscription ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6), [ADR 0010](docs/decisions/0010-letterless-grid-and-private-word-list.md))
- Each player sees the words they have to explain, and only a count of the words hidden from them; the grid stays letterless until a word is guessed, so no word gives away a letter of anyone's own words at an intersection ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6), [ADR 0010](docs/decisions/0010-letterless-grid-and-private-word-list.md))
- `firestore.rules` accept a room moving from `lobby` to `playing` only from its owner and only with at least two players in it, so "the owner starts the game" is no longer the screen's word alone ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- A room takes new players only while it waits in the lobby: someone opening the link after the words were dealt out is told the game has begun instead of being let in, on both the screen and in the security rules. Every word of a started room is hidden from one of the players who were in at the deal, so a latecomer would have been shown the whole word list. Players already in the room still reopen their own link freely ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- A player who joined in the instant between the owner reading the room and writing the deal is shown no word at all, and is told the game is running without them. They are in the room but in nobody's assignment — a race no client can close and no security rule can catch, since the rules cannot walk the `words` map — and without this every word would have read as theirs to explain ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- `guess-checker` module in `packages/shared`: `checkGuess(input, answer)` decides whether a guess spells a word, with the edges trimmed and letter case dropped — so however the owner capitalised a word when creating the room does not decide a game — and nothing inside the word touched ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7))
- Answering words, end to end: a player types letters into the squares of the words hidden from them, and the answer is checked the moment the last square of a word holds a letter — no Enter, no button. A right answer is written to Firestore and appears as letters on every screen in the room at once; a wrong one is shown as refused and taken back off the board, with attempts neither counted nor limited. This closes the game loop: a crossword can now be played from the first word to the last ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- The grid knows which squares belong to a player without knowing what is in them: `gridViewFor` hands over the squares of that player's own words as bare coordinates plus a closure that answers whether a spelling is right, and puts a letter in a square only once a solved word runs through it. Nothing that draws the grid reads `layout` at all ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- Words that cross a solved one fill in for free, and a word its crossings finished off entirely counts as answered by the player it was hidden from — otherwise a word with no empty square left could never be closed, and a dense enough grid could never be won ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- Two words hidden from the same player can cross, and typing now follows the word being filled through the shared square instead of turning down the other one. The word being filled is drawn apart from the player's other squares; clicking a square the cursor is already on — or pressing the space bar — swaps between the two words that cross there, and the swap is explained only to a player who actually has one ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- Words a player explains are crossed out once the player they were hidden from has answered them, so the explainer can see what is left to explain rather than only their own progress ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7))
- The end of a game, end to end: once every word of the crossword is answered the room is marked `completed`, and every screen in it swaps to the finished game at the same moment, without a reload. The finished screen shows the full grid, the crossword's whole word list — no word is a secret once every one of them is in the grid — and that the room did it together; there is deliberately no per-player tally, because a word its crossings filled in is recorded against the player it was hidden from and any such table would lie ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- The game is closed from the room as it arrives, never from the answer a client has just sent: two players answering the last two words within the same second each still see the other's word open, so neither would ever call the game finished and a full grid would sit in `playing` for good. Every client that sees a full board writes the same `status: 'completed'`, which makes several of them doing it at once harmless ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- What spells the crossword out at the end is the board, not the room's status: a `completed` status is a client's word that the security rules cannot check against the `words` map, so a room merely marked finished — a game nobody played — shows no word of its list. Such a room is still closed for good, and says so instead of celebrating ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- Someone opening the link to a room whose game is over is told it is over, instead of being told it has begun and to come back later. Both are refused entry for the same reason, but they are not the same news; players of the room itself still get their finished game back when they reopen the link ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- `firestore.rules` and `firebase.json`: anonymous players may create and read rooms but cannot list, delete, or rewrite the crossword of one; `pnpm test:rules` checks the rules against the Firestore emulator ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4))
- CI `Rules` job: `pnpm test:rules` runs the Firestore security-rules tests against the emulator on every pull request, so the rules can no longer be broken by a green build. `firebase-tools` moved into the repo's dev dependencies, so the command needs no global install ([issue #22](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/22))
- `apps/web` imports `packages/shared` as a workspace dependency (`shared: workspace:*`), proven working end to end: module resolution, TypeScript types across the package boundary, build and tests — not merely two independent packages sitting side by side

- Firebase Analytics reports what the product is doing: a room created, a player joined, a game finished, and a page view for every screen — including the screens reached without a reload, which the SDK's own page view never saw ([issue #11](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/11), [ADR 0014](docs/decisions/0014-telemetry-without-room-ids.md))
- Sentry reports JavaScript errors and unhandled promise rejections, tagged with the deployment they came from. It stays out of the way entirely when there is no DSN, which is the state of a fresh checkout and of every test run ([issue #11](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/11), [ADR 0014](docs/decisions/0014-telemetry-without-room-ids.md))
- Neither of them can name a room. The id in a room's address is the whole access control around the game — whoever holds it reads the room document, every word of the crossword included — and both SDKs would have shipped it without a line of our code: Firebase attaches the address bar to `page_location`, Sentry to `request.url` and to every navigation breadcrumb. Firebase's automatic page view is therefore switched off and replaced by one this app sends with the id taken out, analytics events are typed so that a room id, a word or a nickname does not compile, and every Sentry event — not a list of fields known today to carry a URL — is redacted on its way out. Browser tracing and session replay are deliberately not switched on: the first reports every navigation by address, the second records the DOM the words are drawn into ([issue #11](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/11), [ADR 0014](docs/decisions/0014-telemetry-without-room-ids.md), [ADR 0004](docs/decisions/0004-ui-only-word-visibility.md))
- `firestore.rules` refuse to bring an expired room back to life: expiry is now checked on what the room was as well as on what the write would make it. A room past its 24 hours is one the TTL policy has already been told to collect and may collect at any moment, so a game resumed inside one would vanish mid-word. No client asks — the room screen turns everybody away from an expired room, players included — and the rules refuse it anyway ([issue #9](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/9), [ADR 0013](docs/decisions/0013-keeping-a-room-alive-on-every-write.md))

### Changed

- All repository documentation and code comments are written in English
- The room screen is chosen as a value rather than assembled from flags. `roomScreenFor` composes what the connection, the access rules and the board already know and answers with one of seven screens — connecting, unavailable with a reason, join, lobby, playing, finished, closed early — which `RoomPage` renders through an exhaustive `switch`, so a phase added later cannot be handled in six places and forgotten in the seventh. Each phase is now its own component holding only what that phase can act on: the lobby owns the deal and what to say when it is refused, the game owns answers and the ending it watches for, and the two terminal screens take no callbacks at all, because they have nothing left to call. Nothing a player sees or can do changed; the previous single component decided between four phases through three booleans derived from one status field, of which eight combinations were expressible and four were real ([issue #38](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/38), [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-room-document-schema.md))
- A finished game is now closed only by a client that is actually playing it. The watcher that writes `status: 'completed'` used to run above the access checks, so a stranger holding the link ran it too; it now lives on the playing screen, which only a player of a live game is shown. The one case this drops is a room whose players have all closed their tabs while an onlooker keeps the link open: that room stays in `playing` until the TTL policy collects it, which it already would have done for a room nobody has open at all ([issue #38](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/38), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- Letters typed into the grid but never accepted no longer survive a room being closed with words still unanswered. They were drawn on under a notice saying the room was closed and beside a caption promising that unanswered words stay blank; a game played to its end is unaffected, because every square of a full crossword already holds its solved letter ([issue #38](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/38))

### Fixed

- The owner of a freshly created room can walk into it: the panel that hands them the invite link now also offers a link to the room itself, next to the link and its **Copy link** button rather than in place of them. They were a player of their own room all along — creation writes them into its `players` — but nothing on that screen led there, so the only way in was pasting the URL into the address bar by hand. Nothing takes them in on its own: the invitation is usually sent before its owner walks in, so the choice stays theirs ([issue #27](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/27))
- The lobby no longer implies a game needs four players. A game starts from two, and the room said the opposite in three places at once: one status line about waiting shown to everybody — the owner included, directly above a **Start the game** button they could press right then — a `Players (2 of 4)` counter that read as progress towards four, and nothing at all telling everybody else that the owner is the one who starts. The two roles are now told different things: the owner hears whether they can start and what is missing if they cannot, everybody else hears that the game is the host's to start and so has a reason to wait. Both lines follow `wordAssignmentRefusal` — the same question the start button asks and `assignWords` asks itself — so the screen cannot come to disagree with the button again. The counter is now `Players (2)` with the room's capacity said as a ceiling beside it, and who may start a game, and from how many players, is unchanged ([issue #29](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/29))
- A room's 24 hours now restart with every write to it, instead of being fixed once at creation. `expiresAt` looked like housekeeping for the Firestore TTL policy, but the security rules read it on every update, so a room whose expiry stopped moving stopped accepting writes the moment it ran out: a game played across more than a day died in the middle of itself, refusing answers and unable to be finished. Every update a game makes — a player joining, the words being dealt out, a word being answered, the game being closed — is now built in one place and carries a fresh expiry, and the type of a room update is one only that place can produce, so a write added later cannot quietly skip it ([issue #9](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/9), [ADR 0013](docs/decisions/0013-keeping-a-room-alive-on-every-write.md))
