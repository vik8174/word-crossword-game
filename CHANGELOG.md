# Changelog

Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), versioning follows [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Where people stop is measured. The four screens between opening the app and the first word being explained — the home page, `/create`, the nickname form and the lobby — each report that they were reached, so the drop-off between them is read rather than guessed at. Two of them were already distinguishable by their address; the other two are the point, being two screens at one address that no page view could ever have told apart. An arrival is recognised by the name of the screen and by nothing else: a room screen is rebuilt on every snapshot and a live room receives one every fifteen seconds from each client marking itself present, so anything watching the screen itself would have reported four arrivals a minute at a screen nobody arrived at ([issue #51](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/51), [ADR 0023](docs/decisions/0023-a-screen-name-is-text-that-has-been-redacted.md))
- Analytics events may now carry text, on the one condition that it has been through the redaction. `Redacted` is produced in a single place and nowhere else, so a room id, a nickname, a word or the address bar still cannot be handed to an event as it stands — what replaced "numbers, and nothing else" is "what has not been redacted does not compile", checked by the compiler exactly where the old rule was. The event that carries a screen name narrows it further and takes four fixed words rather than text, so there is nothing a player typed for it to be given ([issue #51](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/51), [ADR 0023](docs/decisions/0023-a-screen-name-is-text-that-has-been-redacted.md))
- Players are shown who is still there. Every client rewrites a mark of its own in the room every 15 seconds, and a player whose mark is more than 45 seconds old is named as away beside their name, with how long it has been — the difference between somebody thinking and somebody gone, which is what decides whether the other one keeps waiting or keeps explaining into nothing. Only that deviation is labelled: a line every row carries says nothing, which is what `Players (2 of 4)` cost. A room that is over shows none of it, since nobody marks themselves present in a finished game ([issue #47](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/47), [ADR 0022](docs/decisions/0022-a-mark-a-player-writes-for-themselves.md))
- A player the room has stopped hearing from is told so, on their own screen. Firestore serves a client its own writes from cache, so somebody whose connection has gone still sees their letters land in the grid and the room exactly as they left it, while the other player talks to nobody. Their own mark in the document they are reading goes stale at precisely the moment their writes stop arriving, because it is one of those writes — the same question, asked about the reader, with nothing taken from `navigator.onLine`, which reports a network rather than a database ([issue #47](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/47), [ADR 0022](docs/decisions/0022-a-mark-a-player-writes-for-themselves.md))

### Fixed

- A guest the room turns away is told about the room rather than about their connection. Joining writes `players.<uid>` without re-reading the room by decision, so the security rules are the only thing standing between two guests reaching for the same free seat — and since a lobby seat frees itself once its mark is 90 seconds old, two people opening the link within the same minute read the same free seat as a matter of course. Whoever was a moment slower was shown _Check your connection and try again_, a network fault reported to somebody whose network was fine. A refusal and an unreachable database are now told apart in one place, by the code Firestore attaches to the failure, and the guest who lost is offered a room of their own instead of a button that would be refused all over again. No cause is named, because none is given: a refused write is answered with `permission-denied` and nothing more, so a room that filled up, a game dealt out while a nickname was being typed and a room past its 24 hours all arrive as the same fact. What is shown says only that much, and gives way to the room's own words the moment the next snapshot lands — the subscription behind the nickname form never stopped running. A join that genuinely could not reach the database still says so, word for word as before ([issue #50](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/50), [PRD #31](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/31))
- One stray tab no longer costs the whole game. Opening the invite link on a second device signs that browser in as a player of its own, so a host checking their own link filled the room and the friend it was sent to met a wall — with no way out, since nothing removed a player, a room cannot be deleted and its 24 hours were the only exit. A visitor now takes a lobby seat whose mark is more than 90 seconds old, in the one write that also puts them in, so the room is never a size the security rules refuse. Never the host's seat: they are the likeliest to step out, being the one sending the link, and they alone can start the game ([issue #47](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/47), [ADR 0022](docs/decisions/0022-a-mark-a-player-writes-for-themselves.md))
- `firestore.rules` hold a room's players frozen from the deal onwards: a player key can no longer disappear once a room is `playing` or `completed`. The check asked only that the keys after a write be among the keys before it, which refuses an arrival and permits a departure — and a player dropped from a game leaves their words hidden from nobody, so the crossword can never be finished and there is no way back, `completed` being terminal and the room undeletable. The lobby stays open, which is where a seat that was given up can change hands ([issue #47](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/47), [ADR 0022](docs/decisions/0022-a-mark-a-player-writes-for-themselves.md))
- `firestore.rules` refuse to let the host be taken out of the room they made, in any state. A lobby's roster is otherwise every client's to write — which is what lets an arriving player take a seat nobody is sitting in — so without this the one seat presence never frees could still have been taken by anybody holding the link ([issue #47](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/47), [ADR 0022](docs/decisions/0022-a-mark-a-player-writes-for-themselves.md))
- Setting up a deploy no longer costs a failed deploy to find out what the runbook left out. The service account needs a fourth role — Service Usage Consumer — because publishing `firestore.rules` makes the CLI ask whether the Firestore API is enabled, which the three roles listed before did not allow; the failure named neither the role nor the step, arriving as `403 Permission denied to get service [firestore.googleapis.com]` after everything else had succeeded. The deploy also checks the service account key before it builds: that it is JSON, that it is a service account key rather than a field of one or a path to the file, and that it belongs to the project being deployed to — so a key pasted wrongly, or a production key left in the stage environment, is named as such instead of surfacing at the last command as `Failed to authenticate, have you run firebase login?` ([issue #53](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/53))
- Reloading after creating a room no longer loses the room. The room id and the words that built it were held in component state on `/create`, so a reload, a closed tab or a crash destroyed the owner's only way into the game they had just made — the one player who could not get the link back out of an address bar. The owner is now inside the room at its own address, and every screen there is derived from the room document, so coming back to the link is safe at every point of a game ([issue #48](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/48), [ADR 0021](docs/decisions/0021-one-room-address.md))

### Changed

- A room is for two players, and the client, the security rules and every sentence the game shows now say it the same way. The ceiling was four in `MAX_PLAYERS` and in `firestore.rules`, while the game has only ever been played by two — the words are dealt from two players up — so a third guest with the link was told the room was full rather than that a game here is a pair. Both numbers moved in one change because they had to: joining writes `players.<uid>` without re-reading the room, so the rules are the only gate, and a client stopping at two while the rules still took four would have opened a two-seat race on the way in where none existed. The third guest is now told what the game is and offered a room of their own, and the lobby states the size a game is played at instead of naming a ceiling to work towards. Nothing supporting larger rooms was removed — `assignWords` still has no upper bound at all, and [issue #10](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/10) holds the work of lifting the boundary ([issue #49](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/49), [PRD #31](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/31))
- Creating a room takes the owner into it. A room has one address, `/room/<id>`, and creation replaces `/create` with it in history rather than pushing, so Back leads out of the flow instead of to a filled-in word list — going back to one invites creating a second room the guests were never sent to. The invite link travels with the owner: it is on the room screen now, above whatever the room is showing, so every player can pass it on, and it is there while the room is still being read because the link is built from the address and waits for no snapshot. It goes once the words are dealt out, when sharing it would only send a friend to a refusal ([issue #48](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/48), [ADR 0021](docs/decisions/0021-one-room-address.md))

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-18

### Added

- Development process setup: ADRs, CONTRIBUTING, CI plan
- Repository scaffolding: pnpm workspace (`apps/web`, `packages/shared`), Vite + React + React Router + MUI, Firebase SDK (env config), ESLint + Prettier, Vitest with an 80% coverage threshold, GitHub Actions CI (lint/test/coverage) ([issue #2](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/2))
- CI `Build` job (`pnpm build` for both packages), and the workflow now also runs on `push` to `main`, not only on pull requests ([issue #17](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/17))
- `apps/web/src/firebase/config.ts` fails immediately, listing every missing required `VITE_FIREBASE_*` variable, instead of passing `undefined` into `initializeApp` ([issue #17](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/17))
- `crossword-generator` module in `packages/shared`: `generateCrossword(words)` builds an intersecting crossword grid and returns the words that did not fit as `unplacedWords` instead of losing them ([issue #3](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/3), [ADR 0008](docs/decisions/0008-crossword-layout-library-and-contract.md))
- `word-list-validator` module in `packages/shared`: turns the raw text of the word-list field into the words a game is built from, plus every reason the list cannot start one — count, length, alphabet and duplicates ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4))
- Room creation, end to end: the owner enters a nickname and a word list, the crossword is generated in the browser, the room is written to Firestore with the owner already in it as its first player, and the owner gets a link to share ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4), [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-room-document-schema.md))
- Words that did not fit into the grid are shown to the owner for approval before the room is created; a word list that yields no grid at all is refused with an explanation instead of becoming an unplayable room ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4))
- Joining a room by its link, end to end: the visitor is signed in anonymously the moment the page opens — before the room is read, as the security rules require — then picks a nickname and is written into the room's `players` map under their own auth id. The screen follows the room live from then on, so the player list fills in as the others arrive ([issue #5](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/5), [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-room-document-schema.md))
- The room screen draws the crossword as bare squares, with no letters: the room document holds every word in plain text, and nothing may be shown until words are assigned to players ([issue #5](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/5), [ADR 0004](docs/decisions/0004-ui-only-word-visibility.md))
- A link that leads nowhere is explained rather than left blank: an unknown address, a room that was never there or has already been collected, a room past its 24 hours, and a room that already holds four players each say what happened and offer a way on ([issue #5](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/5))
- `word-assignment` module in `packages/shared`: `assignWords({ wordCount, playerIds })` deals a crossword's words out among 2-4 players, hiding each word from exactly one of them and spreading them evenly — including who carries the spare when the words do not divide evenly. Everybody is left at least one word to guess: a crossword with fewer words than players is refused rather than dealt, and `wordAssignmentRefusal` states that condition once, so the start button explains what is missing instead of offering a deal that would throw ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- Starting a game, end to end: the owner deals the words out from two players up, the assignment is written into `words.<id>.hiddenFromPlayerId` in one update, and every screen picks the same deal up from its live subscription ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6), [ADR 0010](docs/decisions/0010-letterless-grid-and-private-word-list.md))
- Each player sees the words they have to explain, and only a count of the words hidden from them; the grid stays letterless until a word is guessed, so no word gives away a letter of anyone's own words at an intersection ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6), [ADR 0010](docs/decisions/0010-letterless-grid-and-private-word-list.md))
- `firestore.rules` accept a room moving from `lobby` to `playing` only from its owner and only with at least two players in it, so "the owner starts the game" is no longer the screen's word alone ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- A room takes new players only while it waits in the lobby: someone opening the link after the words were dealt out is told the game has begun instead of being let in, on both the screen and in the security rules. Every word of a started room is hidden from one of the players who were in at the deal, so a latecomer would have been shown the whole word list. Players already in the room still reopen their own link freely ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- A player who joined in the instant between the owner reading the room and writing the deal is shown no word at all, and is told the game is running without them. They are in the room but in nobody's assignment — a race no client can close and no security rule can catch, since the rules cannot walk the `words` map — and without this every word would have read as theirs to explain ([issue #6](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/6))
- `guess-checker` module in `packages/shared`: `checkGuess(input, answer)` decides whether a guess spells a word, with the edges trimmed and letter case dropped — so however the owner capitalised a word when creating the room does not decide a game — and nothing inside the word touched ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7))
- Answering words, end to end: a player types letters into the squares of the words hidden from them, and the answer is checked the moment the last square of a word holds a letter — no Enter, no button. A right answer is written to Firestore and appears as letters on every screen in the room at once; a wrong one is shown as refused and taken back off the board, with attempts neither counted nor limited. This closes the game loop: a crossword can now be played from the first word to the last ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- The grid knows which squares belong to a player without knowing what is in them: `gridViewFor` hands over the squares of that player's own words as bare coordinates plus a closure that answers whether a spelling is right, and puts a letter in a square only once a solved word runs through it. Nothing that draws the grid reads `layout` at all ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- Words that cross a solved one fill in for free, and a word its crossings finished off entirely counts as answered by the player it was hidden from — otherwise a word with no empty square left could never be closed, and a dense enough grid could never be won ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- Two words hidden from the same player can cross, and typing now follows the word being filled through the shared square instead of turning down the other one. The word being filled is drawn apart from the player's other squares; clicking a square the cursor is already on — or pressing the space bar — swaps between the two words that cross there, and the swap is explained only to a player who actually has one ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- Words a player explains are crossed out once the player they were hidden from has answered them, so the explainer can see what is left to explain rather than only their own progress ([issue #7](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/7))
- The end of a game, end to end: once every word of the crossword is answered the room is marked `completed`, and every screen in it swaps to the finished game at the same moment, without a reload. The finished screen shows the full grid, the crossword's whole word list — no word is a secret once every one of them is in the grid — and that the room did it together; there is deliberately no per-player tally, because a word its crossings filled in is recorded against the player it was hidden from and any such table would lie ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- The game is closed from the room as it arrives, never from the answer a client has just sent: two players answering the last two words within the same second each still see the other's word open, so neither would ever call the game finished and a full grid would sit in `playing` for good. Every client that sees a full board writes the same `status: 'completed'`, which makes several of them doing it at once harmless ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- What spells the crossword out at the end is the board, not the room's status: a `completed` status is a client's word that the security rules cannot check against the `words` map, so a room merely marked finished — a game nobody played — shows no word of its list. Such a room is still closed for good, and says so instead of celebrating ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- Someone opening the link to a room whose game is over is told it is over, instead of being told it has begun and to come back later. Both are refused entry for the same reason, but they are not the same news; players of the room itself still get their finished game back when they reopen the link ([issue #8](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/8), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- `firestore.rules` and `firebase.json`: anonymous players may create and read rooms but cannot list, delete, or rewrite the crossword of one; `pnpm test:rules` checks the rules against the Firestore emulator ([issue #4](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/4))
- CI `Rules` job: `pnpm test:rules` runs the Firestore security-rules tests against the emulator on every pull request, so the rules can no longer be broken by a green build. `firebase-tools` moved into the repo's dev dependencies, so the command needs no global install ([issue #22](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/22))
- `apps/web` imports `packages/shared` as a workspace dependency (`shared: workspace:*`), proven working end to end: module resolution, TypeScript types across the package boundary, build and tests — not merely two independent packages sitting side by side

- Firebase Analytics reports what the product is doing: a room created, a player joined, a game finished, and a page view for every screen — including the screens reached without a reload, which the SDK's own page view never saw ([issue #11](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/11), [ADR 0014](docs/decisions/0014-telemetry-without-room-ids.md))
- Sentry reports JavaScript errors and unhandled promise rejections, tagged with the deployment they came from. It stays out of the way entirely when there is no DSN, which is the state of a fresh checkout and of every test run ([issue #11](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/11), [ADR 0014](docs/decisions/0014-telemetry-without-room-ids.md))
- Neither of them can name a room. The id in a room's address is the whole access control around the game — whoever holds it reads the room document, every word of the crossword included — and both SDKs would have shipped it without a line of our code: Firebase attaches the address bar to `page_location`, Sentry to `request.url` and to every navigation breadcrumb. Firebase's automatic page view is therefore switched off and replaced by one this app sends with the id taken out, analytics events are typed so that a room id, a word or a nickname does not compile, and every Sentry event — not a list of fields known today to carry a URL — is redacted on its way out. Browser tracing and session replay are deliberately not switched on: the first reports every navigation by address, the second records the DOM the words are drawn into ([issue #11](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/11), [ADR 0014](docs/decisions/0014-telemetry-without-room-ids.md), [ADR 0004](docs/decisions/0004-ui-only-word-visibility.md))
- `firestore.rules` refuse to bring an expired room back to life: expiry is now checked on what the room was as well as on what the write would make it. A room past its 24 hours is one the TTL policy has already been told to collect and may collect at any moment, so a game resumed inside one would vanish mid-word. No client asks — the room screen turns everybody away from an expired room, players included — and the rules refuse it anyway ([issue #9](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/9), [ADR 0013](docs/decisions/0013-keeping-a-room-alive-on-every-write.md))

- The crossword is numbered, so a word can be named out loud. `numberCrossword` in `packages/shared` gives every placed word the number a crossword would give it — the squares words begin in, ordered down the grid and then across it, counted from one, with a single number where an across word and a down word begin together — and the number is drawn in the corner of that square on every screen. It is derived from the layout and never stored: the layout is fixed when the room is created, so every player works out the same numbers from the same document and there is nothing to keep in step. It gives nothing away either, since the outlines of the squares already show where each word starts and how long it is. Players who could previously only describe where they meant can now say "four down, six letters, crosses my apple at the second P" ([issue #40](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/40), [PRD #32](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/32))
- The crossword is played from the keyboard. The arrow keys move around the whole board, square by square and over the squares of anybody's word, stepping across the holes a generated grid is full of; a perpendicular arrow turns the corner where this player has a word running that way through the square it arrives at, so the cursor and the next letter never part company; and backspace clears the square it is in, or steps back along the word and clears there when that square is already empty — over the letters written in for this player, which were never theirs to take off the board. The square the next letter will land in carries a ring of its own, drawn apart from the tint that marks the word being filled, so which word and which square are two facts read at once rather than one guessed from the other ([issue #42](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/42), [PRD #32](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/32), [ADR 0016](docs/decisions/0016-the-cursor-lives-in-the-grid.md))
- Tapping a word in the panel takes the player to it in the grid: a word of their own is reached at its first square still open and ready for a letter, a word they explain is brought into view and stays nobody's to type in. For twenty words the grid is wider than a phone, so half the board is off screen and the numbers in it are there to find a word once somebody names it — which is what the panel now does. Only where a word runs crosses from the panel to the grid, never how it reads, so both halves of the panel work the same way and neither can spell anything out ([issue #42](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/42), [ADR 0016](docs/decisions/0016-the-cursor-lives-in-the-grid.md))
- Every square of the grid now says where it is, what it holds and whether it is this player's to fill, and the board is a single stop for the Tab key rather than one stop per square — twenty words being about eighty of them. A player using a screen reader hears the square they arrive at whenever the cursor moves, which is what the cursor leaving the browser's focus and becoming state of the grid had to buy back ([issue #42](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/42), [ADR 0016](docs/decisions/0016-the-cursor-lives-in-the-grid.md))
- An error thrown by the deployed app arrives in Sentry with a stack trace that names a file and a line of source, instead of a column in a minified bundle. `@sentry/vite-plugin` uploads the build's source maps and deletes them from `apps/web/dist` again — Firebase Hosting publishes that directory whole, and a map left in it would hand every player the unminified source, so a guard at the end of every build fails it if any `.map` survived. The upload and the bundle agree on one release name, resolved once from the commit the build was made from and given both to the upload and to `init()`; two names and Sentry would hold maps and events with no way to match them, which reads as a minified trace with nothing saying why. The token that does the uploading is deliberately not `VITE_`-prefixed, since every `VITE_` variable is embedded into the public bundle in plain text, and a build without the token generates no maps at all and still succeeds — a fresh clone and CI on a pull request build exactly as they did before ([issue #52](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/52), [PRD #31](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/31), [ADR 0018](docs/decisions/0018-source-maps-for-sentry-only.md))
- Errors in Sentry are filed under the version they came from, not only the build: the release a bundle reports and files its source maps under is now the product's version joined to the commit it was made from, `1.0.0+700f8a8b3c9d`. The version has a single home in the root `package.json`, beside the tag and the changelog that describe the same repository; the workspace packages keep a placeholder nobody reads. Because the version comes from a file every build already reads, a build can always name itself — so a source archive, which has no commit to read and previously uploaded no maps at all, now uploads them like any other build ([issue #52](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/52), [ADR 0019](docs/decisions/0019-a-release-is-a-version-and-a-commit.md))
- A production environment, on its own Firebase project beside the stage one: two databases, two key sets, two addresses, and a game shared with somebody that no longer shares a database with whoever is breaking a rules change that afternoon. Errors from the two arrive in one Sentry project separated by the environment they carry, which is the tag that already existed because both are built in the same Vite mode and nothing else tells them apart ([issue #53](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/53), [PRD #31](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/31), [ADR 0020](docs/decisions/0020-two-environments-and-a-deploy-that-runs-itself.md))
- Deploying is no longer something to remember: a merge to `main` reaches stage on its own, and a `v*` release tag reaches production, so what is live stops falling behind what is merged — three pull requests behind, at the time this was written. Each deploy publishes the app and `firestore.rules` in one command, because the rules are the whole of the access control in a project with no backend and a database running an app without them is a database with the wrong rules on it. A build is given the secrets of one environment and has no name for the other's, `.firebaserc` has no default project left for a forgotten flag to find, a variable that never arrived fails the deploy instead of the game, and a run is never cancelled halfway through an upload ([issue #53](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/53), [ADR 0020](docs/decisions/0020-two-environments-and-a-deploy-that-runs-itself.md))
- The git tag and the version the repository states can no longer disagree quietly: a tag that does not name the version in the root `package.json` deploys nothing and says which of the two to move — the pairing [ADR 0019](docs/decisions/0019-a-release-is-a-version-and-a-commit.md) left to this release ([issue #53](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/53))

### Changed

- The desktop is the target while the grid does not fit a phone. A square is 32 pixels with a 2-pixel gap, so a crossword of 17 columns is 576 pixels across and one of 22 is 746, against a phone's 375 — between a third and a half of the board is off screen. Reaching a word from the panel made that navigable and did not make it fit. Recorded as a stated limitation rather than left as an unspoken intention, and deliberately not answered yet: whether the phone is served by drawing the board differently or by a room that cannot produce a board this wide belongs to [issue #31](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/31) ([ADR 0017](docs/decisions/0017-desktop-first-while-the-grid-outgrows-a-phone.md))
- The words a player has to explain are written into their own grid, in place, instead of sitting in a list beside a board with nothing in it. An explainer can now see how long their word is, where it sits and everything it crosses, and may say so out loud — which is what makes the thing on screen a crossword rather than a diagram. A square says why its letter is there: answered by the group, or this player's own to explain, told apart by a dashed outline and a slanted letter as well as by a tint, and a word visibly turns plain for its explainer the moment its guesser answers it. Telling those two apart is what keeps somebody explaining a word nobody has guessed yet. The invariant three tickets were built on is inverted rather than dropped, and is asserted the way it is now stated: no word hidden from a viewer appears anywhere in their rendered document — checked over the whole document, against every word the room hides from them ([issue #41](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/41), [PRD #32](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/32), [ADR 0015](docs/decisions/0015-explained-words-in-the-grid.md))
- Between 17.3% and 26.0% of a player's own letters — at two and at four players — now arrive already written, because a word they explain crosses one they must guess, and the most-covered single word in thirty measured grids arrived 67% written. Accepted as measured rather than mitigated: no word was ever covered completely, and a word that arrives free costs one word of play rather than the game. [ADR 0015](docs/decisions/0015-explained-words-in-the-grid.md) carries the numbers and says why each mitigation was rejected ([issue #41](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/41))
- The panel beside the grid is an index rather than a word list: two numbered lists, the words this player explains and the words they guess, each named the way the players say them out loud — "one down", "three across". The second replaces the counter that was there, so a guesser can ask for one of their own words to be explained again by name. Of the words hidden from them it still holds no letter and no length ([issue #41](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/41))
- A letter written into the grid because this player explains the word through it counts towards a word of theirs crossing it, exactly as an answered letter already did — the letter is right and in front of them, and retyping it would be copying what they can read. A room closed before its crossword was finished now says that the words nobody guessed are not spelled out _apart from the ones that were yours to explain_, which were on that reader's screen all along ([issue #41](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/41), [ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-typing-guesses-into-the-grid.md))
- All repository documentation and code comments are written in English
- The room screen is chosen as a value rather than assembled from flags. `roomScreenFor` composes what the connection, the access rules and the board already know and answers with one of seven screens — connecting, unavailable with a reason, join, lobby, playing, finished, closed early — which `RoomPage` renders through an exhaustive `switch`, so a phase added later cannot be handled in six places and forgotten in the seventh. Each phase is now its own component holding only what that phase can act on: the lobby owns the deal and what to say when it is refused, the game owns answers and the ending it watches for, and the two terminal screens take no callbacks at all, because they have nothing left to call. Nothing a player sees or can do changed; the previous single component decided between four phases through three booleans derived from one status field, of which eight combinations were expressible and four were real ([issue #38](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/38), [ADR 0009](docs/decisions/0009-room-document-schema.md))
- A finished game is now closed only by a client that is actually playing it. The watcher that writes `status: 'completed'` used to run above the access checks, so a stranger holding the link ran it too; it now lives on the playing screen, which only a player of a live game is shown. The one case this drops is a room whose players have all closed their tabs while an onlooker keeps the link open: that room stays in `playing` until the TTL policy collects it, which it already would have done for a room nobody has open at all ([issue #38](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/38), [ADR 0012](docs/decisions/0012-ending-a-game-from-the-received-state.md))
- Letters typed into the grid but never accepted no longer survive a room being closed with words still unanswered. They were drawn on under a notice saying the room was closed and beside a caption promising that unanswered words stay blank; a game played to its end is unaffected, because every square of a full crossword already holds its solved letter ([issue #38](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/38))

### Fixed

- The owner of a freshly created room can walk into it: the panel that hands them the invite link now also offers a link to the room itself, next to the link and its **Copy link** button rather than in place of them. They were a player of their own room all along — creation writes them into its `players` — but nothing on that screen led there, so the only way in was pasting the URL into the address bar by hand. Nothing takes them in on its own: the invitation is usually sent before its owner walks in, so the choice stays theirs ([issue #27](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/27))
- The lobby no longer implies a game needs four players. A game starts from two, and the room said the opposite in three places at once: one status line about waiting shown to everybody — the owner included, directly above a **Start the game** button they could press right then — a `Players (2 of 4)` counter that read as progress towards four, and nothing at all telling everybody else that the owner is the one who starts. The two roles are now told different things: the owner hears whether they can start and what is missing if they cannot, everybody else hears that the game is the host's to start and so has a reason to wait. Both lines follow `wordAssignmentRefusal` — the same question the start button asks and `assignWords` asks itself — so the screen cannot come to disagree with the button again. The counter is now `Players (2)` with the room's capacity said as a ceiling beside it, and who may start a game, and from how many players, is unchanged ([issue #29](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/29))
- A room's 24 hours now restart with every write to it, instead of being fixed once at creation. `expiresAt` looked like housekeeping for the Firestore TTL policy, but the security rules read it on every update, so a room whose expiry stopped moving stopped accepting writes the moment it ran out: a game played across more than a day died in the middle of itself, refusing answers and unable to be finished. Every update a game makes — a player joining, the words being dealt out, a word being answered, the game being closed — is now built in one place and carries a fresh expiry, and the type of a room update is one only that place can produce, so a write added later cannot quietly skip it ([issue #9](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/9), [ADR 0013](docs/decisions/0013-keeping-a-room-alive-on-every-write.md))

### Known limitations

Shipped knowingly, ticketed, and named here rather than left for whoever plays the game to find:

- **Reloading the create screen loses the room and the words that built it.** The room id lives in component state and nowhere else, so a refresh between creating a room and sending its link leaves no way back into it — the words are typed again ([issue #48](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/48))
- **The product says it takes up to four players; in practice it takes two.** The word assignment deals from two players up, and the four in `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the notice a turned-away guest reads is the size the game was planned at, not the one it has been played at ([issue #49](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/49))
- **The host's second device joins their own room as another player, with no way to undo it.** Opening the invite link in a second browser signs in as a new anonymous player, which fills a seat in the room the host already owns and cannot be given back ([issue #47](https://github.com/vik8174/word-crossword-game/issues/47))
