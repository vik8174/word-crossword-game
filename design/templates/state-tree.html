The greeting is back to the version already in the code. Plain paper, one line of summary, one action. The component sheet put the played-for words on the cloth and printed the pattern behind them; both cost legibility, and the implemented shape was the better one. The only thing kept from that pass is holding the pattern clear of the centre, where the words sit.

Switch the word list to 18 and go to playing. That is the panel problem, made visible: two long hint paragraphs plus a list per side, in a zone that is 250 px wide and one screen tall. The zones scroll — which is what the app does today — but scrolling is what a player has to do while somebody is talking to them. Worth deciding what gives: the hints, the list, or the width.
